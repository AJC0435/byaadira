A LITTLE CONTEXT

Somewhere between precision and ponder.

"You have to be odd to be number one." - Dr. Seuss

What began as a pull towards the laws of nature has grown into a fascination with the processes that govern the world around us. Having explored around the world of physics for years, I've found my love for the behaviour of light, the technical world of coding, and the extraordinary processes that make us human. That aside, I'm also a total bookworm for horror and science fiction, a lover of the outdoors, and a forever introvert who should probably be cutting back on caffeine and reconsidering their relationship with sleep. Follow this page to know more about me — my work over the years, my interests, and the questions that continue to keep me curious!

Still learning. Always investigating.
